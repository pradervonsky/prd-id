## Introduction

Business Intelligence bridges the gap between modern **data science** and **business applications** by transforming data into insights that enable strategic decision-making.

---

## BI Core Activities

### Extract & Analyse
Extracting and analysing information from data to:
- Predict behaviour
- Recognise patterns
- Generate forecasts

***

### Visualise & Present
Presenting insights through:
- Interactive dashboards
- Executive reports
- Decision-support tools
